NASA Science Classroom — Lunar Dust & Solar Energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into clean electricity using photovoltaic cells. On Earth, wind and rain wash dust away. But on the Moon, there is no weather! Lunar regolith dust is jagged, razor-sharp, and holds an electrostatic charge from direct solar radiation, causing it to cling tightly to glass solar panels.
