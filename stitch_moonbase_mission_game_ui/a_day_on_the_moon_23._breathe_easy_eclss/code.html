4 KEY LUNAR DISCIPLINES
air
MODULE 01

Breathe & Circulate

Without gravity-driven thermal air convection, exhaled carbon dioxide would pool around sleeping astronauts like an invisible bubble. Continuous fans circulate fresh oxygen throughout every corridor.

Airflow: 0.2 m/s Circulating ➔
filter_alt
MODULE 02

Remove Carbon Dioxide

Humans breathe in oxygen and exhale CO2. Advanced regenerative scrubbers and amine filters trap and vent excess CO2 before it can cause headaches or oxygen depletion.

Scrubber: CDRA Active PPM: 320 OK
water_voc
MODULE 03

Recycle Water

Water is super heavy to launch from Earth! Lunar life support captures cabin humidity from breath and sweat, distilling it with urine processor assemblies into ultrapure drinking water.

Recovery: 98.4% Purified ➔
sensors
MODULE 04

Monitor the Habitat

Dozens of sensors constantly watch habitat air pressure (held at 101.3 kPa like Earth's beaches), track O2 partial pressure, and balance thermal radiators against the -130°C to +120°C lunar surface swings.

Atmosphere: 101.3 kPa Nominal ➔

Earth Does It for Free, but Space Stations Must Build It!

"Spacecraft and habitats need carefully managed life-support systems because astronauts cannot rely on Earth's open environment."

On Earth, vast forests, blue oceans, and a huge atmosphere naturally recycle carbon dioxide back into oxygen and filter fresh rainwater. On the Moon, our entire planetary biosphere is engineered inside compact titanium boxes!
