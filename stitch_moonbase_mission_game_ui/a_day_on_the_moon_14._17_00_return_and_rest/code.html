NASA Science Note

Why Astronauts Sleep in Tethered Bags

On the Moon (with only 1/6th of Earth's gravity) and aboard the International Space Station, astronauts use sleeping bags tied securely to the wall. Without tethers, astronauts could float gently around the cabin, bump into delicate computer monitors, or push buttons in their sleep!

lightbulb Fun Fact: Space bunks have small airflow fans so exhaled CO₂ doesn't bubble around the sleeper's face!
